# WHY WOSS NEEDS A HACK CLUB

Most of what I know came from building things with other people online. I want every student at White Oaks to have that same chance, in person, down the hall.

## WHAT WE WILL DO

  • Build a welcoming, hands-on maker culture where every experience level belongs.
  • Ship real hardware and software projects, every week.
  • Run mini-hackathons at school, from a single lunch hour to a full day.
  • Pair newer members with experienced ones for peer-led learning.
  • Connect WOSS to the global Hack Club community and its programs.

## FIRST STEPS

Find a teacher sponsor, set a weekly meeting slot, and open with a beginner-friendly build night so anyone can walk in and leave with something working.
